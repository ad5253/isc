AIM (Experiment 8):
To determine the focal length of a convex lens (B) by combining it coaxially with another convex lens (A), not in contact.
Apparatus: optical bench with 4 uprights, two convex lenses A and B, two pins. Least count: bench scale = 0.1 cm (1 mm)
PART 1 — STEP 1 OF 5
Make the tips level
What is parallax? (30-second idea)
Hold one finger near your face and one far away, then move your head: the near finger shifts more. That difference is parallax.
The lens (or the two lenses) makes an upside-down image I′ somewhere in the air. If I′ forms exactly where pin I stands, both are at the same distance from you, so they shift together and the tips stay joined. If they separate when your head slides, I′ is in front of or behind the pin: move pin I.
