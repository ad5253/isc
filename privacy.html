Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This explains what we collect when you use the ISC Class 12 Study Portal, why, and your choices. It is written to follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. The person responsible for your data is Adnan Merchant, Vapi, Gujarat, India.

What we collect

  • Sign-up details: your name, email, phone/WhatsApp (optional), the payment transaction ID, and the password you create.
  • Use of the Portal: which pages, PDFs and labs you open and for how long, sign-in times, a device identifier, and your IP address. This is used for security and to understand which material helps most.
  • Progress: what you have marked as done.
  • Feedback and requests you choose to send.

We do not collect your bank details or UPI PIN. Payments happen in your own UPI app.

Why we use it

  • to check your payment, create your account and let you sign in;
  • to keep the Portal secure and stop sharing and copying of the material (your name and the time are shown on pages you view);
  • to contact you about your account;
  • to improve the Portal.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Passwords

Your password is used only to sign you in. It is stored in the Portal's private database, which only the administrator can open, and the administrator can reset it. Please do not reuse a password from your email or bank.

Who processes the data for us

The Portal runs on services from Google (Apps Script and Sheets, which hold the account records and email), Cloudflare (speed and security), Backblaze (file storage) and GitHub (website hosting). These providers handle data on our behalf, and some of them are outside India.

How long we keep it

We keep your account details for as long as your account exists. Usage logs are tidied up regularly. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your personal details, except what we must keep for payment records and security.

Children

If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must agree before you sign up and pay, as stated in the Terms.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can withdraw your consent (which ends your access). Contact us on WhatsApp or by email. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Security

We use access controls, signed access tokens and encrypted connections (HTTPS). No system is completely risk-free, but we work to protect your data and will tell you if a breach affects you.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
